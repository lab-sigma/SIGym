Trading Game Analysis¶

This interactive environment simulates a market making game. Your objective as the trader is to maximize your profit against a market maker with the same goal. Every round, the market maker puts out a bid and an ask. You must then respond by buying or selling a number of contracts to them.


Basic Strategies

Let...
\(M_n\): Price set by market maker for round \(n\)
\(I_n\): Expected price calculated by investor for round \(n\)
\(u_p\): Utility for player \(p\)
\(R_n\): Set of cards revealed up to round \(n\)
\(X\): True price

Assume: Bid = Ask = Price (\(M_n\))

Investor's strategy:

if \(I_n > M_n\): then buy $$u_i = \mathbb{E}[I_n - M_n]$$ if \(I_n < M_n\): then sell $$u_i = \mathbb{E}[M_n - I_n]$$

The expected profit of the investor is then as follows:

$$u_i = \mathbb{E}[I_n-M_n] \times P(I_n > M_n) + \mathbb{E}[M_n-I_n] \times P(I_n < M_n)$$

Let \(q = P(I_n > M_n)\)
Simplified,
$$u_i = \mathbb{E}[I_n-M_n] \times q + \mathbb{E}[M_n-I_n] \times (1 - q)$$ $$ = \mathbb{E}[I_n \times q - M_n \times q] + \mathbb{E}[M_n(1 - q) - I_n(1 - q)]$$ $$ = \mathbb{E}[2I_nq - 2M_nq + M_n - I_n]$$ $$ = \mathbb{E}[I_n - M_n](2q-1)$$

Fair game:

For \(I_n = \mathbb{E}[X|R_n]\), \(M_n = \mathbb{E}[X|R_n]\): $$u_i = \mathbb{E}[\mathbb{E}[X|R_n] - \mathbb{E}[X|R_n]](2q-1)$$ $$ = \mathbb{E}[0](2q-1)$$ $$ = 0$$

With market maker information advantage:

For \(I_n = \mathbb{E}[X|R_n]\), \(M_n = \mathbb{E}[X|R_{n+1}]\): $$u_i = \mathbb{E}[\mathbb{E}[X|R_n] - \mathbb{E}[X|R_{n+1}]](2q-1)$$ $$ = (\mathbb{E}[\mathbb{E}[X|R_n]] - \mathbb{E}[\mathbb{E}[X|R_{n+1}]])(2q-1)$$ $$ = (\mathbb{E}[X] - \mathbb{E}[X])(2q-1)$$ $$ = 0$$

Given a perfectly optimal player, the utility of both the market maker and investor will be 0.
